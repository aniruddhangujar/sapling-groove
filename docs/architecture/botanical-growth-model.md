# Botanical Growth & Vitality Model

> *"A tree does not lose its rings because winter was long."*

Most productivity applications implement binary streak systems. If a user practices daily for thirty days and misses Day 31, their counter drops to zero. This mechanistic design induces anxiety, encourages dishonest check-ins, and prompts users to abandon the tool when a streak inevitably breaks.

Sapling Groove replaces punitive streak counting with a **dual-axis biological growth model**.

---

## 1. The Dual-Axis Philosophy

Human dedication fluctuates with the seasons of life. To reflect this reality, the system decouples **historical progress** from **current practice rhythm**.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE DUAL-AXIS LIFECYCLE                         │
├───────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┤
│         ACCUMULATED WORK          │          CURRENT VITALITY          │
│       (Permanent / Immutable)     │       (Fluid / Responsive)         │
├───────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Every focused minute is banked  │ • Reflects recent practice rhythm  │
│ • Trees never shrink in stature   │ • Healthy when watered regularly   │
│ • Growth milestones are forever   │ • Enters dormancy/wilt if neglected│
│ • Cannot be lost or erased        │ • Rapidly recovers upon return     │
└───────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. Botanical Life Stages

Every intention planted in the Grove progresses through distinct morphological stages based on accrued focus minutes:

1. **Sprouting Seed**: The initial intention breaks ground; delicate shoots appear in the soil.
2. **Sapling**: Stems strengthen; initial foliage emerges and responds to sunlight.
3. **Branching Cultivar**: The core trunk thickens; distinct species morphology (e.g., Oak, Pine, Willow, Bamboo) becomes clearly visible.
4. **Canopy Maturation**: Leaves densify, bioluminescent spore particles float from the branches, and root structures ground the specimen into the terrain.
5. **Eternal Canopy**: Once target hours are achieved, the tree enters the **Permanent Canopy**. It is permanently thriving, immune to future neglect, and stands as a lifelong monument to deep work.

---

## 3. Non-Punitive Wilting & Recovery

When an active intention is neglected for multiple days, the tree does not vanish. Instead, it enters a state of **botanical wilting**:

### The Invariant Floor
- **Structural Integrity**: The tree's physical height, branch count, and accrued focus minutes remain 100% intact.
- **Health Floor**: Vitality can never drop below a hard floor (15%). Even after months of absence, a tree is never dead; it is merely resting in drought.
- **Visual Adaptation**: Foliage gently shifts from vibrant bioluminescent emerald to muted earthy amber tones, and branches lower slightly to signal that water is needed.

### The Return to Practice (Recovery Curve)
When a user returns to their ritual, the recovery model actively rewards the gesture:
- A single meaningful session halts wilting immediately.
- Dedicated focus over subsequent days triggers accelerated chlorophyll restoration.
- Returning is framed as an act of nourishment, not an admission of failure.
